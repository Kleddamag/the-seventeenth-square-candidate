# Endpoint Case-G contradiction (named composition lemma)

This is an explicit restatement and application of an **already independently accepted** analytic lemma, not a new unreviewed numerical claim. The original proof is endpoint-normalization-producer/THEOREM.md, sections 1–2, and the independent proof is independent-endpoint-normalization-review/REVIEW.md, first section. Their exact identities appear below.

Let L be the algebraic S* defined by the accepted root gate. Let seventeen closed unit squares with pairwise disjoint interiors fit in [0,S0]^2 with S0<L. Put d=(L-S0)/2>0 and translate every square by (d,d). Call the resulting packing K. Every point of K lies in [d,L-d]^2. This remains true after a common D4 symmetry about the centre of [0,L]^2. In particular no square in any such image touches an L-wall.

Suppose that an injective labelling of this endpoint packing, in one such common image, satisfies the literal JG_exact_v2 root. The reviewed local result T_JG puts its sixteen constrained parents in G20S in the rational reference-coordinate system. Apply the accepted **physical** G20S theorem with actual container side L (hence actual S<=S*). It yields zero for all 45 exact-root core coordinates. The rational coordinates used to state the input window are not substituted into this conclusion. In the exact-root family the nonslider role 8 has centre (L-1/2,L-1/2) and axis (1,0). Its right edge is x=L and its top edge is y=L. This contradicts K subset [d,L-d]^2. Under a common reflection/rotation these contacts remain contacts with actual endpoint walls. Consequently a padded smaller packing cannot belong to Case G once its root capture hypotheses are supplied.

The equality conclusion S=L alone would be tautological for K and is not the contradiction. The wall-contact conclusion is essential. No assumption about force, minimizer attainment, contact graph, uniqueness of the free square or slider stop attainment is used. The seventeenth square remains an actual distinct parent; G20S simply needs no condition on label 4 beyond what the packing already supplies.

For completeness, in the fixed capture frame with U=1169/250 the pose-centre identity is

p + (L-S0)/2 (1,1) + (U-L)/2 (1,1) = p + (U-S0)/2 (1,1).

Thus padding changes none of the fixed-U predicates or their simultaneous physical-parent assignment. The later conversion to local rational reference coordinates changes descriptions, not the physical side L. Root admission must use this exact endpoint conversion; it is separately pinned in the manifest.

This closes the analytic Case-G implication **conditionally on capture and the accepted local premises**. It does not assert that all packings have already been captured. No further charge certificate for the algebraic endpoint is needed.
