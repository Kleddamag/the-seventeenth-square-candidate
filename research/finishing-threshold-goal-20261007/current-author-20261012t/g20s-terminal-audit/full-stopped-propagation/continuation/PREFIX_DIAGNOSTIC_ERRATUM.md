# Scope correction for the raw product diagnostic

The frozen first-three-round report correctly computed differences between
all48 lifted product variables and products of their saved floating factors.
Its largest raw differences involve a*x41, z*x41 and a*x49. Those products
are absent from all52 geometric rows; they occur only in their own product
hull rows. Their large differences cannot by themselves identify a loss
affecting a geometric objective, or justify splitting a rather than b/z.

Thus the prefix's suggestion to use its largest raw product differences to
prioritize a subsequent inequality or partition should not be relied on.
Only products that occur in the geometric rows can support that diagnosis.
The continuing exact side-interval contraction remains a separate, valid
reason to test further full propagation in a separate experiment.

No bound, model, exact residual, saved vector or source in the sealed prefix
is changed by this correction. The continuation uses exactly the same245
necessary rows and no new product inequality or parameter split.
