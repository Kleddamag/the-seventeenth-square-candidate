# Conventions and names

This glossary fixes names; literal class and root files remain the geometric definitions.

- **Physical parent / role / label:** a parent is one of the original seventeen unit squares. A role is a region-identified place in the capture argument. A numerical label becomes a parent label only after the stated uniqueness/assignment proof. The construction-label permutation in Section1 concerns the reference pattern, not a silent assignment of arbitrary packings.
- **L, R, C, U:** algebraic optimal candidate; larger rational tree cap; charge cap; fixed frame side. The notation `L_k` means a loose-square class statement, not a side. `C_j` means a capture statement, not the scalar cap. The class prefix `Q` denotes a low-wall profile, not the checked centre cell also denoted Q in the score proof.
- **All-direction marker / half-marker:** the former is the hexagonal centre region with all square directions; the latter uses that same region with direction representative in[0,pi/4]. Only the all-direction pose set is invariant under the common vertical reflection. `M_marker` denotes the applicable high pose class; `M` in a budget ratio is the total integer charge budget.
- **Charge inner core / rigid-layout core:** a charge core is a closed set strictly inside one unit square used to count sites. The45 rigid-layout core coordinates are29 position deviations plus16 angle deviations after excluding three slider coordinates; these have nothing to do with charge cores.
- **Three angle charts:** global class angles use t=tan(theta/2) with quarter-turn representatives written in the file; canonical direction gates use specified representatives modulo pi/2; the local tree uses sine/cosine of rotation relative to each fixed reference basis. The exact admission theorem converts complete arcs between these charts, including sign conventions and chart poles. It does not equate their numerical parameters.
- **WC / AQ / S_k:** WC is the literal low wall-column pose alternative in the tilted files; AQ is its smaller closed wall pose used in the L files (rectangle displayed in Section5); S_k is the all-direction loose target for label k. They are different sets. Off-wall WC and AQ are empty because each lies in the unique L9 column.
- **TT:** the union of tilted trigger pose classes in the literal PT statement. An actual failed gate supplies a parent there; the union itself need not have capacity one.
- **4/4/0:** two high classes of threshold4 and a low union of threshold0, evaluated by the minimum containing threshold (default 1) on physical parents, not summed per slot.
- **PB / HULLW:** names of the baseline rigid target and the admitted enlarged wall-based target; they specify closed pose sets through their pinned class files. HULLW11 and PB11 both fit the selected v5 root factors. The labels are not additional theorems.
- **PM / PT / PS / PSS / PSE / PW:** class families respectively using a marker-only implication, a union of tilted triggers, a specified loose trigger, that trigger restricted by its additional L premise, one signed trigger end, or the wall trigger. Their precise thresholds and low targets are the literal pinned files. A family name never stands in for a budget receipt.
- **Slab factor:** the special label11 position/angle target forced by the selected SL11_T020 statement in the middle-wall case; its inclusion is in JPWS_v4. It is not a replacement of all label11 factors in other branches.
- **FRAME_L9 counts:** N(V) is the number of physical parents in V. In either first pair statement, m is the number in its high marker class, t is the number in its low union, and q is the number in both. Thus t−q counts low parents outside the high class. In pair09_17, h counts parents in the union of all its high classes; the reviewed separation makes its low union V disjoint, so the low count is N(V). All counts are of parents, once per parent.
